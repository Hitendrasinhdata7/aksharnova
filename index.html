How we work

A clear process, from idea to launch

Six steps that keep projects predictable, transparent and moving forward.

Step 1 of 6

Discover

01

We start by understanding your goals, users and constraints — before any design or code.

What we do

  • Stakeholder and user interviews
  • Review of existing systems and data
  • Define success metrics and scope

What you get

  • A clear problem statement
  • A prioritised list of opportunities
  • A realistic scope and roadmap

Step 2 of 6

Design

02

Architecture and UX are planned together, so what we build is both sound and simple to use.

What we do

  • Solution architecture and technology choices
  • UX flows and clickable prototypes
  • Security and integration planning

What you get

  • An architecture blueprint
  • An interactive prototype to react to
  • A delivery plan with milestones

Step 3 of 6

Build

03

We engineer in short iterations, with working software you can see at every stage.

What we do

  • Iterative sprints with regular demos
  • Code review and automated testing
  • Continuous integration from day one

What you get

  • Working increments each sprint
  • Tested, documented code
  • Transparent progress reporting

Step 4 of 6

Integrate

04

We connect your new product to the systems, data and AI models it depends on.

What we do

  • API and third-party integrations
  • Data migration and pipelines
  • AI and LLM integration with evaluation

What you get

  • Connected, end-to-end workflows
  • Validated data flows
  • Performance and accuracy checks

Step 5 of 6

Launch

05

A secure, staged release with monitoring in place from the very first day.

What we do

  • Security review and load testing
  • Staged rollout and go-live support
  • Monitoring and alerting setup

What you get

  • A production-ready release
  • Runbooks and team handover
  • Hands-on launch support

Step 6 of 6

Evolve

06

We stay involved after go-live, improving the product with real usage data.

What we do

  • Track usage and performance
  • Prioritise improvements with you
  • Ship enhancements regularly

What you get

  • A continuous-improvement roadmap
  • Ongoing support and maintenance
  • Room to scale as you grow
